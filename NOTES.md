## The thermometer

Swedish's letters are closed. The sharp probe is the letters Swedish does
not have:

- **Danish / Norwegian** — `æ ø` (they write these where Swedish writes `ä ö`)
- **German** — `ß ü`

**`å` is shared by all three Scandinavian languages and is never
flagged.** Swedish's own `ä ö` are never flagged.

## The Name

Swedish tradition renders יהוה as **HERREN** — in small capitals, in
Karl XII:s bibel, Folkbibeln and Bibel 2000 alike, set that way precisely
to mark that the Name stands beneath it. This chair writes **Jahve**, and
keeps D1 for the rest: Elohim · Adonaj · Shaddaj · Jah · El · Tsevaot.
`gud` and `herre` keep their lawful seats (the nations' gods; a human
lord) — the probe is token-guided, never a word-list, and matches STEMS
(`herre → herren → herrens`), not citation forms.

**Jahve verses on the full tree: 5,797.**
